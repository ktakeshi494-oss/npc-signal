Public signal journal

Every alert we sent, with its price at the time. After 24 hours we record the outcome if the market has already resolved, and after 7 days we check the price again and record what happened, whether it looks good for us or not. Raw data: journal.json. Updated {{UPDATED}}.

How grading works: for large trades and price moves we compare the outcome price 7 days later with the price at the alert. "Continued" means it moved 2+ points the same way, "reversed" means 2+ points the other way, otherwise "flat". 24h check: only markets that already resolved within 24 hours get a result; "won" means the market settled in the direction of the alert (an up alert ended at 100¢, a down alert at 0¢), "lost" means it settled the other way. Open markets are not graded at 24h. This describes what the market did. It is not a trading result, it ignores fees and slippage, and it is not financial advice.
